What I did

I ran it from the first plan to the last guest leaving: the venue, production, catering, the programme, and an external PR agency. Along the way I kept SehaGroup's CEO up to date with status presentations and supplier price comparisons, so decisions were made on real options. We also prepared the presentations for the apps SehaGroup launched that day.

Not everything went perfectly. The client wasn't happy with the screen quality on the day, so I went back to the production supplier and got a discount.
